I am cutting the Alphabet position from roughly USD 50,000 to USD 7,000.

The thesis is not broken. This is a sizing correction.

I still like Alphabet. Search, Cloud, Gemini, Android, Waymo, TPUs and the broader possibility of a connected AI system remain attractive. What I no longer like is having USD 50,000 tied to one company when I do not think Alphabet deserves that much more weight than the other strongest ideas in the portfolio.

Microsoft is the clearest comparison. I currently see Microsoft as at least as strong a long-term AI-era position, with a cleaner enterprise monetization path through Azure, Microsoft 365, identity, data and agent infrastructure. Keeping Alphabet at USD 50,000 while Microsoft sits around the normalized pilot size makes the portfolio say something much stronger than my actual view.

I had already intended to reduce Alphabet before today and simply failed to execute it. That is an execution mistake. I want that recorded instead of pretending the decision only appeared after the stock moved.

USD 7,000 keeps meaningful exposure if the Alphabet thesis works while stopping one position from dominating the portfolio and the benchmark-relative result. It also puts Alphabet much closer to Microsoft in size, which better reflects how I currently rank the two.

This is not a bearish call on Alphabet and it is not an attempt to predict the next one or two percent of price movement. If Alphabet rises immediately after the trim, that does not by itself make the decision wrong. The question here is position size, not whether the next daily candle is green or red.

I would consider adding again if either the valuation becomes materially more attractive or new evidence raises Alphabet's expected return enough to justify a larger position relative to Microsoft and the other available opportunities.
