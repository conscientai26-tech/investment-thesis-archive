# Amazon (AMZN) — Add Decision — 2026-09-01

## Decision
Add **€1,000** to the existing Amazon position now and keep a further **€1,000** in reserve rather than adding the full €2,000 immediately.

## Context
Amazon fell sharply on 2026-09-01 shortly after the initial starter position had been opened. The decline does not, by itself, invalidate the original investment thesis centered on AWS, AI infrastructure, capital deployment, and Amazon's ability to build an increasingly integrated infrastructure stack.

## Reasoning
The key distinction is between **price weakness** and **thesis weakness**.

A lower share price improves the entry point if the underlying thesis remains intact. However, there is no reliable basis for assuming that Amazon cannot fall further in the short term. Adding the entire remaining €2,000 at once would therefore remove optionality without materially improving the expected long-term thesis exposure.

The preferred structure is:

- Existing Amazon position: approximately **€3,000**
- Additional purchase now: **€1,000**
- New total exposure after this addition: approximately **€4,000**
- Remaining reserve for a further decline or stronger confirmation: **€1,000**

## Why this sizing is preferred
The staged addition creates two useful outcomes:

1. If the current decline proves temporary, the portfolio participates with a larger position.
2. If Amazon falls materially further while the thesis remains intact, the remaining €1,000 can be deployed at a better price.

This is not an attempt to predict the short-term bottom. It is a position-sizing decision designed to preserve flexibility while increasing exposure to a thesis that has not materially weakened.

## Conditions for the remaining €1,000
The final reserve should be considered only if at least one of the following occurs:

- Amazon falls materially further without deterioration in the core thesis.
- New evidence strengthens the AWS / AI infrastructure / capital-efficiency thesis.
- Valuation becomes more attractive relative to expected long-term cash generation.

The reserve should not be deployed merely because the share price is lower. A meaningful deterioration in AWS growth, AI infrastructure economics, capital returns, or Amazon's competitive position would require reassessing the thesis first.

## Status
**Action selected: add €1,000 now; retain €1,000 reserve.**
