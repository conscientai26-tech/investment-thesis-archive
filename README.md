# Investment Thesis Archive

Independent public-equity research, economic frameworks, forward-looking technology theses, and dated virtual portfolio decisions. Research began in July 2026. The goal is to preserve **what I believed, when I believed it, why I believed it, and what happened afterward**—including rejected investments and mistakes.

> **Language / Sprache:** This GitHub overview is in English, but **many original Notion research notes are written in German (Deutsch)**. Some GitHub essays are in English, others in German. The Notion links below lead to the original research in its original language; they are not English translations.
>
> **Portfolio status:** Investment allocations and transaction records in this archive refer to a **virtual / paper research portfolio**, not verified live-money trading. Earlier alpha figures (including the historical 11.9% calculation and the October 14 recorded-reference 10.18% reconstruction) are **not current verified performance**. The owner's latest reported estimate is **approximately 9%**, but its exact value, valuation date, execution assumptions, and reproducible calculation have not yet been supplied or independently verified. Do not cite 12% as current alpha; preserve older figures only as dated historical estimates and methodological corrections. See the portfolio history and methodology below.

## 5. Original-language notes, dates, and provenance

The GitHub files sometimes consolidate or expand ideas **after** their original formulation in Notion. Where a dated Notion source is available, it is linked above to make that development inspectable.

- **Original language:** Many linked Notion pages are in **German**; titles and summaries here are in English for navigation. Some English-language GitHub theses expand on originally German research.
- **Date discipline:** A date in the document title, a GitHub commit date, a Notion page edit date, and the date a particular sentence was first written are different facts.
- **Evidence quality:** Original page history, prior exports and independent archived versions provide stronger chronology than a page's latest edit time alone.
- **No hindsight rewriting:** An updated or later-expanded thesis should be marked as such, with earlier judgments and changing assumptions preserved wherever possible.
- **Open hypotheses:** Forecasts are labeled as forecasts until defined observations establish whether they were right, partly right or wrong.

This repository is intended to make the reasoning auditable—not to turn every analysis into a success story.
