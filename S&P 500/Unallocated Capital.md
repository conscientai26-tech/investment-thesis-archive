# Note on Unallocated Capital

If this were real money, I would not leave most of the unused capital in cash while waiting for new ideas. I would probably keep it in the S&P 500 and only move money out when I find a stock I think can beat it.

I am not doing that in this track record because it would distort what I am trying to measure.

The point here is to see whether the stocks I actively choose outperform the S&P 500. If I put all unused capital into the index, a large part of the portfolio would automatically produce around zero alpha. That would pull down the reported alpha even though my actual stock picks had not become worse.

So the unused part of the $1 million portfolio should not be read as cash I would hold in real life. It is just not included because I want the alpha number to reflect the investments I actually chose.
