## What is working, and what remains uncertain

1. **Established distribution/monetization cores:** Microsoft and Meta have been significant positive examples, consistent *so far* with the portfolio thesis that existing AI-adoption channels may monetize faster. Price action over weeks or months alone cannot identify how much is AI fundamental progress versus broader market and valuation moves.
2. **EDA bottlenecks:** Synopsys and Cadence, initiated in the Sep 8 record, have strong indicative returns. This lends near-term evidence to a bottleneck selection, not yet proof of durable outperformance after proper benchmark attribution.
3. **Enterprise workflow and information assets:** Salesforce and WKL show positive *proxy* price changes, but their entry prices remain to be verified, while SAP is modestly positive and UiPath/Pegasystems are negative.
4. **Culture/content versus enterprise/core:** Netflix's negative price change is consistent with a less immediately rewarded selection, but does not validate a general economic law about cultural businesses.
5. **Concentration in ideas:** Several holdings share assumptions about AI monetization, workflow control, data/execution scarcity, or the preservation of professional knowledge margins. The count of tickers overstates independence of theses.
